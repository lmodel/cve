# Policies

* [AI covenant](ai-covenant.md) - The rules for AI-assisted work here: whoever submits owns the contribution, an element says what the format says, a rule states only what the upstream states, a mapping is read at its source, and no commit carries an AI co-authorship trailer.
* [Licence and attribution](license-and-attribution.md) - The repository's licences and what it takes from elsewhere: Apache-2.0 for the schema and code, CC BY 4.0 for the bundle, CC0 1.0 for the format's files, FIRST's licence on the CVSS files, and the Terms of Use for real records. No endorsement is implied.
* [Security policy](security-policy.md) - How to report a vulnerability in this repository, by GitHub's private reporting, what executes here and what holds each surface. A vulnerability a CVE record describes belongs with that record's publisher.
